# Findings: earnings calls against reports, UBS and JPMorgan 2023–2024

Status 2026-09-24. Descriptive unless a test is named; sources in brackets.

## 1. Main finding

Of the 39 genuine UBS targets set in the calls after the Credit Suisse acquisition, 9 have a figure that appears in no UBS quarterly, annual or Pillar 3 report up to the end of 2024 (`03g_fls_models.ipynb`, `data/phase3d/counterparts_read.csv`, `data/phase3g/new_units_read.csv`, `03f_pillar3.ipynb`). Four are not mentioned in any report: the AT1 issuance of up to 2bn in 2024 (T33), the HoldCo reduction to around 90bn (T68), a going concern capital ratio of around 18% built with AT1 (F11), and an LCR below the 4Q24 level of 188% (F22), a weak case because 188% is the current level and the target only a direction. Five are mentioned without a figure: the Non-core and Legacy (NCL) operational risk RWA of around 14bn (T16) and credit and market risk RWA below 40bn (T27), the NCL capital release of over 6bn (T25), the going-concern requirement rising to 16.7% (T32) and the Group LRD reduction of over 100bn (F12, the reports name only the NCL part). Of the remaining 30 targets, 23 have the same figure in a report, 6 appear with another figure, shown separately and not counted as call-only, and one is the integration-cost total (`03g_fls_models.ipynb`). As before, and counted separately, the expected total of integration-related expenses (around 13bn, raised to around 14bn in 2024-Q4) is stated explicitly only in the calls; the reports give quarterly amounts, next-quarter expectations and, in 2023-Q2, an indirect order of magnitude (`03b_call_vs_report.ipynb`, `data/phase3b/comparison.csv`).

## 2. Method

The 39 targets are 31 from the rule-based extraction of phase 3 and 8 from 22 further commitments that FinBERT-FLS found and the rules missed (`plans/phase_3d.md`, `plans/phase_3g.md`). Search terms were fixed from the call sentence before searching, and every counterpart quote is checked against the report page. A report sentence that speaks in forward-looking form about the same reduction, also with another scope or without a figure, counts as "mentioned without a figure"; Roman set this rule after reviewing the borderline cases, so it was fixed after the results were known (`plans/phase_3d.md`, `plans/phase_3f.md`). Roman checked all nine units: for the three from phase 3g the hits were pre-sorted by machine and he read the borderline cases and a sample of the pre-sorting (`data/phase3d/roman_check/`, `data/phase3f/roman_check/`, `data/phase3g/roman_check/`).

## 3. Pattern

Where the reports address these topics, they say that something is planned or expected, while the calls say how much: capital "released from the unwinding" of NCL against "over 6 billion", a phase-in of higher requirements from 2025 to 2030 against "around 180 basis points to 16.7%", "Reduce RWA and LRD" as an NCL priority against 14bn of operational risk RWA and over 100bn of LRD, a revised estimate of integration costs against "around 13 billion" (`03d_counterparts_read.ipynb`, `03g_fls_models.ipynb`, `03b_call_vs_report.ipynb`).

## 4. Side findings

- **Disclosure channel.** All nine figures come from the prepared remarks, not the Q&A (the three phase 3g units by `data/phase3g/set_s_read.csv`), as do 29 of the 31 phase 3 targets; for two targets the figure came in the call about three and five months before it appeared in a report (CET1 around 14 %, tax rate around 40 %; checked by Roman) (`03e_disclosure_channel.ipynb`).
- **Novelty.** UBS novelty fades faster than JPMorgan's, but the result is fragile and reflects format and speaker change rather than content (`02_novelty_drift.ipynb`).
- **Drift.** Call similarity falls over time in wording (TF-IDF) but not in meaning (sentence embeddings) (`02_novelty_drift.ipynb`).
- **First Republic.** JPMorgan's event-call figures for restructuring costs and incremental net income are never restated as numbers, separate disclosure ends after 2024-Q1, and the calls keep the preliminary 2.7bn gain after the supplements revised it to 2.8bn (`03b_call_vs_report.ipynb`).
- **Automation.** Neither pre-specified rules nor a language model without training reliably identify which figures appear only in the calls or where management evades; FinBERT-FLS adds commitments the rules miss, but only 22 of its 73 additional sentences are new commitments, found by reading (`03g_fls_models.ipynb`); the deflection detector fails the pre-registered validation gate (precision 0.45 at UBS, 0.59 at JPMorgan), so P1-1 and P1-2 are not interpretable and the labels show no reliable difference between the banks (`01_evasive_answers.ipynb`, `03c_call_figures_in_reports.ipynb`, `plans/phase_1.md`).

## 5. Limits

- Eight earnings calls per bank, so every test rests on very few units (`plans/phase_1.md`, `plans/phase_2.md`).
- The main finding concerns one bank: the JPMorgan supplements (Exhibit 99.2) contain no forward-looking statements and offer no comparable written record (`03b_call_vs_report.ipynb`, `03d_counterparts_read.ipynb`).
- Classifications in phases 3b, 3d and 3g were made by one reader; Roman checked the nine units of section 1 and the pre-acquisition programme, for the phase 3g units only the borderline cases after machine pre-sorting; the other units are unchecked (column `checked_by_roman`). "Not mentioned" means not found with the pre-specified search terms listed per unit, not proven absent (`plans/phase_3d.md`). In Roman's blind spot check of ten FinBERT-FLS sentences the genuine/not genuine reading agreed on 9 of 10; he labelled all ten genuine, so kappa (0) is uninformative, the one disagreement would not add a new unit, and the duplicate assignment was not checked (`03g_fls_models.ipynb`, `data/phase3g/spotcheck_comparison.csv`).
- Five of six UBS Pillar 3 reports from 2023-Q3 to 2024-Q4 were searched; none states any of the nine figures, and T16, T25 and T27 are mentioned there without a figure; the 30 June 2024 report is pending for all units in its window (`03f_pillar3.ipynb`, `03g_fls_models.ipynb`). The integration-cost total was not searched in the Pillar 3 reports. The investor presentations belong to the call side, as the transcripts refer to the slides, and were not checked separately.
- Only the deflection sample was labelled; the question topic, novelty and commitment samples will not be labelled, so the topic label, the novelty measure and the commitment extraction are not validated (`plans/phase_1.md`, `plans/phase_2.md`, `plans/phase_3.md`).
- All values rest on the frozen release `data/results/2026-09-23`; the control run of all phases on the final release of the pipeline is still outstanding (`analysis/config.py`, `plans/phase_3.md`).
